## Legal Protections

This site publishes truthful information derived from public records about matters of public concern. It is protected by:

- **First Amendment** — freedom of speech and press
- **Michigan UPEPA** — Uniform Public Expression Protection Act (anti-SLAPP, effective March 2026)
- **Fair Report Privilege** — MCL § 600.2911
- **Absolute Defense of Truth** — truthful statements about public figures acting in public capacity
