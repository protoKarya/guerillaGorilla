guerillaGorilla treats **legal existence as infrastructure**, the same way barraCuda treats GPU compute. Seven subsystems compose into a self-sustaining accountability platform:

1. **Identity** — sovereign legal identity, credentials, standing
2. **Rights** — constitutional protections, FOIA, anti-SLAPP, fair report
3. **Finance** — cost asymmetry, fee waivers, metabolic efficiency
4. **Protection** — First Amendment, Michigan UPEPA, legal shields
5. **Navigation** — venue selection, jurisdiction mapping, filing strategy
6. **Accountability** — evidence curation, public record, provenance chains
7. **Formalization** — pattern documentation, reusable templates, STRIDe cadence

The pattern was discovered through constrained evolution — the same mechanism that drives primal specialization in the ecoPrimals ecosystem. Environmental constraints (adversarial, time-pressured, high-stakes) produced the same three-layer Provenance Trio architecture because that architecture is the **minimal structure for maintaining provable claims over time**.
