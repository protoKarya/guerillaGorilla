Two million years ago, a primate on the African savanna developed an unusual hunting strategy. It could not outrun its prey. It could not outfight its prey. It could not ambush — the savanna offered no cover. Instead, it walked.

The prey sprinted. The primate walked. The prey sprinted again. The primate walked. The prey overheated — quadrupedal locomotion at sprint speed generates heat faster than four-legged respiratory systems can dissipate it. The primate, bipedal, upright, sweating through exposed skin, thermoregulating while moving, maintained pace. The hunt ended not with a kill shot but with metabolic collapse. The prey fell over. The primate was still walking.

This is **pursuit predation**. It made *Homo sapiens* the apex predator on a planet full of things faster, stronger, and more dangerous.

## The Mapping

The same optimization structure independently emerged in:
- Pro se legal strategy
- OSINT analysis
- Regulatory complaint campaigns
- Institutional accountability work

The mapping is not metaphorical. It is structural:

| Biology | Accountability |
|---------|---------------|
| Bipedal thermoregulation | Low operational cost (pro se, FOIA, public records) |
| Quadrupedal sprint heat | High adversary response cost (attorneys, PR, motions) |
| Endurance tracking | Persistent documentation over months/years |
| Metabolic collapse | Adversary resource exhaustion or exposure |
| Savanna (no cover) | Public record (nowhere to hide) |

## The Terminal Posture

The end state is not a confrontation. It is **Positional Patience** — landscape saturation does the work. The operator maintains constant metabolic cost while every adversary response increases theirs.

Every filing they answer costs money. Every FOIA they fight generates records. Every motion they file becomes documentation. Every denial becomes evidence. The landscape fills with their own responses until the record speaks for itself.

## Why This Is Infrastructure

Pursuit predation is not a tactic. It is the **biomechanical substrate** that makes the sensory triad (fEAR · preSCENT · STRIDe) energetically viable. Without metabolic asymmetry, accountability work is a sprint — and sprinters lose to institutions.

With it, the system is self-sustaining: adversary actions are fuel, not friction.
