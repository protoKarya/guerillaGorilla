## The Name

The name operates on three layers simultaneously:

**fEAR** (outward) — The emotional response the system produces in the adversary. The system converts the adversary's own actions into prosecution fuel. Every move they make strengthens the case against them.

**f(EAR)** (inward) — The function of the ear. A sensory organ. The system listens. It does not argue. It does not threaten. It does not negotiate. It hears what the adversary says, files, submits, and does — and processes the signal. `f(EAR)` is a function that takes what the ear collects and returns evolved case state.

**fEAR** (recursive) — The system is fearsome *because* it listens. Silence is more threatening than argument because silence means processing. Every adversary action creates signal. Every signal evolves the case.

## Biological Basis

Hearing is:
- **Event-driven**: sound arrives in discrete packets (filings, actions, statements)
- **Receive-only**: the ear does not broadcast — it collects
- **Long range**: sound travels further than smell or sight
- **Omnidirectional**: the ear catches signals from any direction
- **Always on**: you cannot close your ears the way you close your eyes

## Legal Instantiation

| Input | Source | Processing |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Court filings | PACER, county clerk portals | Docketed, timestamped, cross-referenced |
| OSINT signals | Public records, social media, corporate filings | Pattern-matched against known entities |
| Adversary actions | Motions, responses, communications | Converted to evidence — every response is signal |
| Institutional records | FOIA, state databases | Archived, BLAKE3 hashed, CAS anchored |

## The Conversion

The critical insight: **adversary actions are fuel, not friction**. 

When the adversary files a motion to dismiss, fEAR hears it as a signal revealing their legal strategy. When they deny a FOIA request, fEAR hears it as evidence of institutional resistance. When they hire an attorney, fEAR hears it as confirmation that the system has produced the emotional response its name describes.

The adversary cannot win by acting, and cannot win by not acting. Action creates signal. Inaction creates timeline. Both evolve the case.
