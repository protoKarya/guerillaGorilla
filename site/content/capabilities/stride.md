## The Name

**STRIDe** — Strategy, Tactics, Reconnaissance, Intelligence, Deployment, execution.

The lowercase 'e' is deliberate. Execution is functionally the least important component. It is directionality — the foot hitting the ground. Everything before it is the real work.

## Concept

fEAR senses and builds. STRIDe moves.

Sensing without motion is surveillance. Building without purpose is accumulation. The system needs a **gait** — a continuous, adaptive, self-correcting process that translates intelligence into action.

A stride is not a step. A step is discrete — it starts, it ends, it succeeds or fails. A stride is continuous. It adapts to the sidewalk. It doesn't falter at every crack. You catch yourself in stride when you stumble. The ground changes under you and your body adjusts before your conscious mind registers the change.

## The R⇄I Loop

The core of STRIDe is the **Reconnaissance ⇄ Intelligence** feedback loop. Reconnaissance collects raw signal. Intelligence processes it into actionable understanding. The loop runs continuously — not in phases, not in sprints, but as a sustained metabolic process.

| Component | Role | Cadence |
|-----------|------|---------|
| **S**trategy | Long-term objective | Monthly revision |
| **T**actics | Specific maneuvers | Weekly adjustment |
| **R**econnaissance | Raw signal collection | Continuous |
| **I**ntelligence | Signal → understanding | Continuous |
| **D**eployment | Staging and preparation | As needed |
| **e**xecution | Action | When ready |

## Filing Cadence

In legal application, STRIDe produces a filing cadence that is metabolically efficient:

- **One filing per week** maintains constant pressure at minimal operator cost
- **Each filing builds on the last** — the adversary must respond to an evolving narrative
- **No filing is wasted** — every submission advances the R⇄I loop regardless of outcome
- **Denial is information** — a rejected motion reveals the court's reasoning

The adversary must respond to each filing individually. Their cost per response exceeds the operator's cost per filing. Over weeks, this creates the metabolic asymmetry that pursuit predation requires.
