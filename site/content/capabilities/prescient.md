## The Name

**preSCENT** operates on two layers:

**Olfaction** — Smell is the oldest sense. It detects gradients — not discrete events (that's hearing), but ambient state. Something is *off*. The air has changed. An institution's behavior shifted before any filing appeared.

**pre-SCENT** — The scent comes *before*. preSCENT is the detection of change that precedes visible action. Board meeting agendas that shift focus. Staffing changes at regulatory agencies. Campaign contribution patterns that predict judicial behavior.

## Biological Basis

Olfaction is:
- **State-driven**: smell measures ambient concentration, not discrete packets
- **Gradient-based**: the nose detects *changes* in state, not absolute values
- **Bidirectional**: you both detect and emit scent — your presence is broadcast
- **Close range**: smell attenuates with distance — it requires proximity or wind
- **Persistent**: scent lingers after the source departs

## The Bidirectional Property

This is the critical difference from fEAR. The ear only receives. The nose also *emits*.

**detroit.primals.eco is preSCENT** — it is ambient presence broadcast. The site exists. The evidence is public. The record accumulates. Everyone in the network can *smell* it — they know it's there, and they know it grows.

The site doesn't need to be actively promoted. Its existence changes institutional behavior. Board members who know their connections are documented behave differently than those who believe they're invisible.

## Network Mapping

preSCENT's graph analysis capability maps the entity network — not just who is connected, but how connections *change over time*. The typed edges in `data/edges.toml` with epistemic grammar create a relational map where every connection has a confidence level and a source document.

The network map is not static intelligence. It is a **living gradient** — new connections appear, old ones shift, and the topology reveals structure that no single document contains.
