## The Name

**amicusContra** — *friend of the court, against unaccountable power.*

The traditional amicus curiae ("friend of the court") provides perspective. amicusContra goes further — it is the outward projection function of guerillaGorilla, extending accountability infrastructure toward cases the operator is not personally party to.

## Three Directions

| Direction | Target | Action | Metaphor |
|-----------|--------|--------|----------|
| **Downward** | Those beneath | Stabilize — prepare rooms, infrastructure as service | The gorilla lifts |
| **Lateral** | Those who are like | Cross-protect — mutual defense, shared intelligence | The gorilla stands beside |
| **Upward** | Those above | Hold to account — force reproducibility, same standard as science | The gorilla challenges |

## Downward: Prepare Rooms

Provide infrastructure to those who need it but cannot build it themselves:
- Templates for pro se filings
- FOIA request frameworks
- Evidence organization patterns
- The dispersal pattern itself — *anyone can produce a .primals.eco site*

The rooms are prepared before anyone asks for them. When they arrive, the infrastructure is already there.

## Lateral: Cross-Protection

Work with those fighting similar battles:
- Shared OSINT databases
- Cross-referenced entity networks
- Mutual citation and evidence corroboration
- Three-surface evidence replication across independent operators

Lateral projection creates **resilience through redundancy**. No single operator's work depends on any other's infrastructure, but all benefit from the shared graph.

## Upward: Hold to Account

Apply the same standard to institutions that institutions claim to apply to individuals:
- **Reproducibility**: if a court claims a process was followed, reproduce it
- **Documentation**: if an agency claims compliance, request the records
- **Verification**: if an institution claims oversight, verify the oversight
- **Publication**: if the record contradicts the claim, publish the record

The standard is not adversarial. It is **scientific** — the same reproducibility standard that validates a physics experiment validates a judicial process. If the result cannot be reproduced from the stated method, the method is suspect.
