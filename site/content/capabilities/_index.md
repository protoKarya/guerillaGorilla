guerillaGorilla coordinates sensing and action through modalities named from evolutionary biology. Each maps to a biological function, a range, and a legal instantiation.

| Sense | Name | Mode | Range | Legal instantiation |
|-------|------|------|-------|---------------------|
| Hearing | **fEAR** | Event-driven, discrete, receive-only | Long | PACER, OSINT, court filings |
| Olfaction | **preSCENT** | State-driven, gradients, bidirectional | Close | Behavioral shifts, institutional weather |
| Gait | **STRIDe** | Continuous, proprioceptive | Self | Filing sequence, route planning |
| Projection | **amicusContra** | Outward, directional | Variable | Cases operator is not party to |
