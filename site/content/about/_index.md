## What This Is

guerillaGorilla is a **methodology meta-primal** in the [ecoPrimals](https://sporeprint.primals.eco) ecosystem. It is not a binary you compile — it is a coordination pattern that emerged when cryptographic provenance infrastructure was applied to public accountability under real operational pressure.

## Where It Came From

The pattern was **discovered, not designed**. It appeared independently in legal work (10+ cases, 124+ archived artifacts, 2 Michigan counties) without reference to Papers 20–22 where the Provenance Trio was first formalized for scientific computing.

This makes it the **fourth independent instantiation** of the same three-layer architecture:

| Domain | Source | Discovery |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Science | ecoPrimals Papers 20-22 | Designed for reproducibility |
| Gaming | Session management | Designed for attribution |
| Medical | Clinical protocols | Designed for compliance |
| **Legal** | **Operational pressure** | **Discovered under constraint** |

The fact that the same architecture emerged independently under adversarial legal pressure — without reference to the formal pattern — is evidence that the Provenance Trio is **domain-independent infrastructure**, not a science-specific design.

## The ecoPrimals Connection

guerillaGorilla sits in the ecoPrimals catalogue as `EntityKind::Methodology` alongside 15 primals (infrastructure binaries) and 8 springs (science validation environments). It demonstrates that the ecosystem's patterns generalize beyond science to any domain that produces typed, citable claims.

The infrastructure that runs this site — Forgejo, Zola, Caddy, golgiBody, BLAKE3, WireGuard — is the same infrastructure that runs [primals.eco](https://sporeprint.primals.eco). The evidence layer IS the infrastructure layer.
